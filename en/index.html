About me

I spent six years as a radar systems technician and non-commissioned officer in the French Air and Space Force, maintaining air-defence weapon systems. In 2025, I left it all behind to pursue what I really cared about: video editing.

The rigour and attention to detail the military taught me now go into your projects. Adverts, YouTube content, corporate videos or UGC — I commit fully to every project, from the first cut to the final render.

UGC is a field I genuinely enjoy and one that suits me: I like being in front of the camera and speaking with sincerity. I have already worked with around a dozen brands on UGC adverts for their social media.
